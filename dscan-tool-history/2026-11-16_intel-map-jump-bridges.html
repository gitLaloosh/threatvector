Why

  • The owner: "add jump bridges to the map (as a setting that can be toggled off and on). when turned on, jump ranges will be calculated using bridges. default setting will be off." and "ideally i'd like to only have near you not use bridges". At the re-plan: "Both count bridges (Recommended)" (058's Jumps colour and Within N jumps), "Switch governs both; gates until 057 (Recommended)" (Threat Vector's route stays by stargate until build queue 057), "Raise to 670 (Recommended)" (the Intel info panel's cap).
  • ESI has no route that lists an alliance's bridges, so the list is pasted (the research note says how other tools get theirs).

What changed

  • Intel settings (the gear) → Jump bridges, right after Routes: "Use jump bridges on the map" (off by default) with the count, a help line, a text box and a row per line: ✓ counted with its distance in light-years, ⚠ counted with why (the same system on two lines; more than 5 LY apart), a dim row for the same bridge again either way round (counted once), ✗ not counted. Kept in dscanIntel as bridges and bridgesOn.
  • The reader, itBridges(): the system names on a line (1–3 words, any case); on a line with an arrow or » the bridge is the last name before it and the first after it, so EVE's structure name ("A » B - name"), another tool's export (with a structure id first, one line per direction) or a row copied with more columns all read; without one, exactly two names.
  • The map's search: itm.Dg by stargate always, and while the setting is on itm.D / itm.par over itBridgeAdj() (stargates plus bridges both ways). itm.jbKey is in the map's key and the match set's key; a change searches and lays out again.
  • Counting bridges: the reach, reports and labels, Jumps from you (colours and numbers), Within N jumps, the hover's "N jumps" with "1 of the 3 jumps by jump bridge · by stargate only 10 jumps", the search box. By stargate always: Near You, its alerts, the Feed and its kill range (the hover's kill-range line too), the alert glow and the fade past Near You's range, Paths to you, and Threat Vector's route (the hover says "route by stargate" and compares it with the stargate count).
  • The bridges are drawn as green curves (--it-bridge, the game's #00f000) while counted; a route step that isn't a stargate is drawn as its bridge's curve (for 057).
  • Sov upgrades' Jump bridge kind (053's hook sovBridgeEnds()) now lists both ends of every counted bridge, with the switch on or off.
  • Info text: intel [Jumps and your system] says the map's counts add your bridges with the setting on (643 of 670 words); check_subtext.py's Intel cap 620 → 670. METHODOLOGY.md: a Jump bridges paragraph and three sentences that said every count is by stargate.
