Lesson for future models

  • No new lesson. Worth knowing: a row's time (st.t) is also its life in Near You and on the map, so a tag that refreshes the time has to carry the newest real report separately (st.rt); otherwise a bubble line would keep a hostile row alive (and alerting) after its pilots were forgotten.
