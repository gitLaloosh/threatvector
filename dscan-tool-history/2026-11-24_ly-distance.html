Lesson for future models

  • LESSON #180: the plan's mutant "the raw LY instead of the rounded one" could never fail. The range is set in whole tenths, and rounding up to a hundredth and then comparing with a whole number of hundredths is the same test as comparing the raw value. The check mutates what can differ instead (rounding to the nearest), with a system just past a tenth in the test world. Before relying on a mutant, name an input where it gives a different answer.
