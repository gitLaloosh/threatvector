Pilot Dossier and System Analyzer: hulls newest first; the login survives the return from EVE

Page 2026-11-11 · v2026.11.11.4 · build queue 037

Why

  • Project doc roadmap/dossier-hull-order-plan.md. The owner: "if a specific hull hasn't been specified when the search was initiated, sort the results by most recent activity. some of the results come back with a hull being shown first that the pilot hasn't used in years in some cases simply because they used to get a lot of kills with it." After a worked example of a 48-hour blend: "we should just list strictly by recency."
  • Also in this delivery (flagged for whichever item shipped next): the bug "sso_game_check times out after Continue to EVE (fails on main too)", and the footer's METHODOLOGY.md link that 036 added (the owner: "i dont want that link displayed").

What changed

  • Hulls newest first (pdRows, pdLast): the dossier's rows run by when each hull was last used, the count only breaking a tie. In All that is the newest kill or loss in it; in Solo, Small gang or Fleet, their newest kill of that size, and the row's last-used column follows the size (a pinned hull with none in that size shows "—"). The dossier opens on the hull used last; a hull asked for (Fleet tab, typed, a System Analyzer row) stays pinned first, its rank now its place by recency.
  • System Analyzer: a pilot row's possible ship (after Intel's pairing and a ship of theirs on your D-Scan, unchanged) is the hull of their newest kill in their gang size. Its hover: "The hull of their newest … kill: …" / "The hull they used last, all sizes: …". More rows may say fit unknown (a recent hull often has no loss yet): the owner accepted that ("that's fine if it's more accurate to what they're likely flying").
  • "Show N more hulls" has no line beside it any more (the owner: "just dont include the extra descriptive text"); "Show fewer" keeps its line.
  • Info text: dossier (the order) and pilots (Possible ship); METHODOLOGY.md's Possible ship and Pilot Dossier lines; CLAUDE.md's "8 busiest".
  • The login after Continue to EVE (LESSON #157): back from EVE while logged in, the online check could renew the old login while the new code was being swapped; the new login then landed and the renewal, seeing another login, ended it and revoked the new refresh token (the user logged out). Now esiFetch's login branch waits for the return (sso.retP) before asking for the token; a renewal answered after a newer login replaced it is dropped without revoking anything; and esiFetch checks the token's permission again once it has it, so a caller that waited sends nothing it isn't allowed (SSO-07).
  • The footer's "How the numbers are worked out" link is gone; METHODOLOGY.md stays in the repo and on the site (the owner: "Just remove the link").
  • Nothing else: no new request, no stored data, the kill list under a row, the counts, the Pilot Analyzer's own guess from losses.

Checks

  • dossier_check.py: expected order worked out from the killmails' times; a new pilot (an old busy Sabre, a Curse used yesterday, a Vedmak) for All, Solo (by the newest solo kill, its last-used column) and Small gang; mutants: the busiest-first sort, the last-used of any size in a gang size.
  • dossier_pin_check.py: the 8 newest; the Ferox kill moved to 21 days ago so the pinned hull is still outside them (10th of 23); "—" for a pinned hull with no kill in the size; no line beside "Show N more hulls".
  • sa_size_check.py: the newest solo and fleet hull (the Sabre, newer than the busier Loki and Purifier), the new hover; the D-Scan ship still first; a busiest-first mutant fails.
  • sso_game_check.py: the stand-in holds the code's answer while the online check asks (no renewal of the old login, the new login stands, nothing revoked); a renewal checked after a newer login replaced it (dropped, nothing revoked); a mutant of each fails. outside_links_check.py: no METHODOLOGY.md link in the footer.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #157: two things that each replace the login (the return from EVE, a renewal) must never run at once. The later one waits for the earlier; a stale answer is dropped, never acted on. Test a race by holding the stand-in's answer, not by timing.
