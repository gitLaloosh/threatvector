The Wormholes tab, part 1 (and three flagged fixes)

IntelWormholesMatchup 2026-11-14 · v2026.11.14.1 · build queue 048 · Project doc roadmap/wormhole-mapping-plan.md ("Build in parts" 1)

Why

The owner: "1. own wormholes tab, 2. yes as you suggest, 3. right after intel", and "add a "BETA" tag on the wormholes tab. i think it's going to take several iterations to get this one right." Part 1 is the tab, its map and tracking; the system menu, lock, Signatures, Share and Routes are parts 2–4.

What changed

  • The Wormholes tab right after Intel, BETA. Your chain is recorded from every reading of your system (the Local log; EVE location, also read while the tab is in front with Intel off): a change with a wormhole system on either side links it to the system you were last in (the owner at the review: "which hole you came through comes from your last recorded location"), except a change that lands you docked by EVE's answer (the owner: "yes, skip system changes that land you docked"). Entering J-space switches Intel to Wormholes; from another tab the button gets a mark; leaving switches back. Stored as tvWhChain; nothing expires.
  • The map: the whole tab, pan, zoom, drag, Shift / Select for a group, an overview, a dot grid; the Intel map's own corner buttons, the gear (Rearrange as a tree) and the ⓘ with the colour key. Focus and the System panel (class and effect from CCP's data, statics coloured by where they lead, who's there, notes, connections); Signatures, Routes and Local say they come later. + Add and "Add a system here".
  • CCP's wormhole systems (INTEL_WH, 2,604 systems, about 13 KB gzipped) written by tools/jump_table_update.py from the SDE, so the daily workflow keeps them current. Read from build 3698585 (the owner downloaded it; the build session can't reach CCP).
  • Intel in a hole counts jumps from where you came in (D14; the owner: "as long as the wormhole tab's routing feature to come counts wormhole connects like jumps, i think that's fine.").
  • Phones: the More menu (D21) keeps the tab row on one line.
  • Flagged with this delivery: the Damage Application card starts at the threat's optimal (turrets, sentries; the owner: "we should have set it to their optimal range."), missiles, Vorton and smartbombs as before; kills are their own route avoid option (ESS stays with hostiles and neutrals, the owner: "ESS by itself indicates someone is in it"); no search box on Paths to you.

Checks

wormhole_tab_check.py (new, with mutants); route_planner_check.py, paths_to_you_check.py and damage_chart_check.py updated.

Lesson for future models

A file-writing tool can trim a line's trailing spaces: the names list for check_pii has two such lines, so check its fingerprint after writing it (the build queue README says so; it happened again here). And a Local log read at Intel's start is a reading too: the tab's first reading came from the folder scan, not the tick, or the jump into the first hole had no system to come from.
