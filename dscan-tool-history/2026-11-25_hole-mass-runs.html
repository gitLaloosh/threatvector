What changed

  • The odds split in two, named by side (hmNext: stuck and col = risk − stuck; hmRiskWords, one helper for the three notes): "about 1 in 131 that it closes with Megathron stuck in J102057; about 1 in 4 that it closes on Megathron's jump in: everyone's home, but the hole is gone, not crit". Roll shows only the stuck part. Crit ranks the least stuck first. The two parts always add up to 070's number (the check works it out for every step).
  • The safe mass (hmSafeTxt): Crit out, a lone ship cold out and back, under Rmin / 2; Roll out, Rmin; coming in, no sentence. 070 subtracted the group's own way back, so it said "No ship is light enough to be sure." beside a safe "Send Thorax instead".
  • + Finisher in the run's pinned line: an inline ship row, "It's in" with the finish side picked, Add when its masses are known. Up to 5 ships. A "Send X instead" per finisher, lightest first; one sent frees only it.
  • Ship lost in the pinned line: if it jumped, answer the step first, then mark it lost; lost before jumping, it leaves the step, and the others of a group step that jumped wait for their answer (held, not worked out again); lost off the finish side, its pod (CCP's Capsule mass, one mode) can come home as a step of its own and leaves the run once home. A lost ship takes no part in any step, odds or "stranded"; its setup row goes (Undo puts it back). A ship added on Change → Carry on asks which side it's in (a replacement), and is never matched to a lost one.
  • Saved runs (tvWhMassRuns): every change is an entry in the run's log (s, add, free, carry, lost), saved at once and replayed from the start (hmRunPl), never the possible holes themselves. Undo goes back through the whole run (past an added finisher, a ship lost and Carry on, each putting the setup's rows back). Opening Mass resumes the newest unfinished run on one of the focus system's connections; Saved runs (N) lists them all, with Open and Delete (it asks). A finished run stays until deleted ("Delete this run" in its end note).
  • WH-01 (a new code group, "Wormholes tab"): a refused save shows the page's warning row in a red box inside the pinned line, so it stays in view; deleting another run saves the open one again and the warning goes.
  • The setup's connection list no longer says "mass mass <50%"; the + Finisher and Ship lost rows repaint when their masses arrive; the Finisher tick reads "kept for the end, offered when it's the safe choice"; whmap#mass reworded (207 → 208 of its 210 words, [Ship lost] and [Saved runs] added).
  • Phone: the pinned line's five buttons in a 3-column grid (two rows, 44 px); on a wide panel they wrap.
  • The Wormholes tab is out of beta: the Beta tag, its CSS rule and the "(beta)" label are gone; the tab is named by its own text, as Intel's.
