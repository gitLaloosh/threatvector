  • At the build review (2026-11-25) nothing needed deciding: 065 and 068 were built as the plan expected; the owner: "yes".

What changed

  • The filter: a new Only filter Activity (past hour) (IT_MAP_FILTERS act, last in the Only group, after Region pick; IT_MAP_ONLY), its icon a pulse line of our own drawing, its settings button "Activity (past hour): which kind and how many".
  • The data: no request of its own. 065's reader (itLv, itLvNeed) is wanted for the picked kind only while the filter is on and the live map shows (itMapLvWant: jumps for Jumps, kills for Ship + pod kills and NPC kills); asked when the filter or its kind changes (itMapLvAsk) and after each draw, as 065's colours are.
  • The match (itMapMatch, itActIx, itActCount): at least N of the picked kind; ship + pod kills add the two; a system missing from ESI's list is 0; no list yet: nothing but your system. Worked out once per key (itActKey: the kind, its minimum, whether the list is there, the read), in both itMapMatch's key and itMapKey.
  • Its settings (058's panel itMp, IT_MP_KEYS): the kind as radios (Jumps · Ship + pod kills · NPC kills, itMpRadHtml), "At least" (RS.mapAct, on IT_ACT_STEPS 1–2000; a typed number goes to the nearest step), a note, the count. Each kind keeps its own minimum (IT_ACT_DEF: 50, 1, 200); switching kind shows that kind's number. Switched on the first time, they open (seen.act).
  • The Only line (itActOnlyText): "at least 50 jumps in the past hour (N) · ESI HH:MM" (N: the systems on the map with that many; the EVE time ESI's counts are as of), "activity: no data yet".
  • The hover (itActTipHtml): the system's count of the picked kind, unless Jumps or Kills (past hour) is the colour, whose own line already says it.
  • Found at the build: a filter panel's count ("N systems match every Only filter that is on") was written only when a setting changed, so a read that landed after the panel opened (065's lists, 068's fights) left it stale. itMapDraw now writes it after working out the match (only while the live map shows, only when its words change).
  • Stored: tvMapStack gains only.act, act {k, j, sp, npc} and seen.act. A stack from before reads with Activity off and the defaults; a bad kind or an off-step number falls back; the page from main drops the new fields and keeps the rest.
  • CARD_INFO.intel [Map]: "Activity: at least N jumps or kills in the past hour"; the Intel cap 776, from the final count. METHODOLOGY.md: Activity (past hour).
  • Checks: tools/page_checks/activity_filter_check.py (new; 13 mutants); the filter lists in intel_map_stack_check, live_map_filters_check (the column's rows, 18), sov_upgrades_check, intel_map_look_check (16 phone buttons) and paths_to_you_check.
  • Found at the full run: with 16 filters, a 1440 x 1000 window's map (661 px) holds 17 of the column's 18 rows, so the column wraps a second column there, as 065 built it for short maps (at 1280 x 800 it wrapped before this change too; 1920 x 1080 holds all 18). Paths to you moves to the top of the second column. Three checks that measured a one-column list were made to read the page's own rows. For the owner to judge at the hand test.

Lesson for future models
