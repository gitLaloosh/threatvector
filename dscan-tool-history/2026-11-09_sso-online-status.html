Show when I'm online in EVE

Page 2026-11-09 · v2026.11.09.1

Why

  • Build queue 029, Project doc roadmap/sso-online-status-plan.md (the clickable prototype stays in the Project). The owner: "I'd like to have the online status reflected in the pilot's bio picture as well", and from the prototype's options, "A and C": a dot on the portrait's corner and the portrait greyed out while logged off.
  • When Intel runs and the character is logged off, the owner chose "Line + a Stop button": Intel never stops by itself ("there is a switch for a reason").

What changed

  • Show when I'm online in EVE (the profile, Pilot, under the EVE login): a checkbox. Without EVE's permission it shows Continue to EVE / Cancel (Phase 2's step), asking for esi-location.read_online.v1 plus what the tab already holds and what's on. Back from EVE it turns on only with the permission (else SSO-07). Under it: "Online in EVE · checked hh:mm UTC" or "Logged off in EVE · …".
  • The portrait at the top right: a green dot on its top-right corner when online; a grey dot and the portrait greyed out when logged off. Intel's square stays on the bottom-right corner. Nothing when the box is off, the tab isn't logged in, or the login lacks the permission. The profile button's label says "online in EVE" / "logged off in EVE".
  • Intel, running as that character, logged off: "Your character is logged off in EVE." with Stop Intel, above the amber "click anywhere" line. Alerts carry on until it's pressed.
  • One tab asks per character: a Web Lock per character (the tab running Intel takes it over and asks on Intel's tick); the others hear the answer and show it; a tab opened later asks for the last answer at once. A tab in the background without Intel asks nothing. The lock is let go before the page leaves for EVE's page and when the page is hidden for good, and a tab that waited 3 minutes without hearing an answer takes over (the tab holding it went quiet).
  • Pace: never before the answer's Expires and 60 s at least (about once a minute); twice as long while under a quarter of the "char-location" tokens are left; Retry-After on 420/429. 401: SSO-08 with Log in again, never asked again; 403: SSO-07, the box off.
  • Log out turns it off (the owner, at the review: "yes"), as it does Use my game location.
  • The budget panel's ESI list gains "Your online status".

Kept in the browser

  • localStorage gains tvOnlineShow (true or false). An older page ignores it; going back to it leaves the key unused. No other key changes form. What EVE answers stays in memory (DLA 9.5).

Checks

  • New sso_online_check.py, on Phase 2's stand-ins (CCP's login with keys made per run, the made-up Chatlogs folder) plus a stand-in for ESI's /online/ (recording each request's headers; Expires, rate-limit headers, 401, 403, 429). It covers the row and its info panel, the permission step and the scope asked for, the headers sent, the 60 s floor, the portrait (dot position, colours, greyed, label), no redraw for the same answer, the slow-down, 429, 401, 403, off, Log out, Intel's line and Stop Intel, the order with the wake line, another character as You, two tabs (one request per period; the other shows it; a later tab hears it at once; hidden; the Intel tab taking over; letting go before leaving; taking over after 3 minutes), the dot and Intel's square at 1440 and 390 px in each Intel state, and 44 px buttons at 390 px.
  • check_request_gates.py: ssoOnCheck listed as an ESI caller with the login; the 401 rule holds it.
