Lesson for future models

LESSON #154: a merge that keeps whatever the newer source lacks also keeps what the newer source removed. The fixture refresh kept fixture-only effects on purpose (live-ESI captures have things an SDE build lacks); after a patch that moved two hull bonuses to new effects, it kept the old ones too, and the Cerberus and Hawk fixtures carried both bonuses — the comparison with pyfa caught it, the pins alone would have been re-pinned to wrong numbers. Tell "the source never had it" from "the source dropped it" with the previous source (OLD_PYFA_SRC), and only keep the first.
