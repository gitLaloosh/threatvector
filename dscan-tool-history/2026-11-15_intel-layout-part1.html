The Intel tab's new layout (part 1) and three UI rules

Intel 2026-11-15 · v2026.11.15.3 · build queue 052 · plan roadmap/intel-layout-sov-plan.md (part 1; mockup Claude outputs/intel_layout_mockup.html, round 6b)

Why

The owner asked for a design cycle on the whole Intel tab before adding a second filter with a panel (Sov upgrades, build queue 053): "i'm wondering if the intel map needs to be more like the wormholes map. incorporate near you into a bigger map, place the feed below it." Six mockup rounds later he picked layout A with the filters "Down the left", the feed icon for Near You ("feed is the one, badge stays"), the site's panel style for every panel over the map, and checkboxes in the text field's colours ("cyan tick, dark box"). At the build review: "1. your choice is fine, 2. yes, 3. sure, 4. leave it shut" (Paths to you re-fits unless moved by hand; centring uses the part no panel covers; Near You and a filter's panel take turns when they don't both fit; a map press leaves Near You shut).

What changed

  • The map takes the card's full width and is taller (wide min(720, max(520, w·0.5)), phone min(600, max(440, w·1.45))); the Feed is below it, full width. The phone's List | Map switch is gone (the map always shows).
  • Near You is a panel inside the map behind one map button (the feed icon, our own drawing) in a rail under the zoom buttons. The button carries the number of Near You's rows, amber while one is in your alert range. Pressed, the list (320 px; a phone: the map's width less the rail, under the filter column's button) opens beside the rail; every control stays where it was and clickable. Open or shut is remembered (nearShown, shut by default). A map press on a system leaves it shut.
  • The filters are a column down the map's left edge under the search box (wrapping into a second column on a short map; a phone keeps it shut behind its one button, taking turns with Near You). Paths to you is last now. A filter with a panel (Wormholes' list) has its panel button to its right; the name line sits right of the panel buttons, level with the filter that's on, and hides while a panel is open. The Wormholes list opens right of the panel buttons, level with its filter, inside the map and clear of Near You; a phone gets a sheet over the map.
  • When both don't fit (a window between a phone and about 1000 px wide), Near You and the Wormholes list take turns, as on a phone.
  • Centring (⌖, the first view, a jump, ⤢, a search pick) uses the part of the map no panel covers.
  • The Wormholes list: each row's system name is a button ("Show on the map") that centres the map on that system in the part the list doesn't cover, its tooltip open; on a phone the list shuts first (the owner: "left click on system name re-centers map on that system").
  • Paths to you fits again when its tree grows or shrinks, unless you zoomed or dragged it since the last fit (the owner: "paths to you map mode should run "fit" every time the layout changes"); its fit leaves room for the filter column and Near You, and ⤢ no longer keeps 40 px for a bar at the foot.
  • Three UI rules in CLAUDE.md, checked by check_ui_rules.py (7-9): A, buttons vs map controls (map controls are neutral, never amber but for the alert-range badge); B, panels over the map (--panel, a --line border, rows --panel-2: Near You's list adopts it); C, one checkbox and one radio in the text field's colours, checked a cyan tick or dot on the dark box (switches excepted).
  • The Intel example follows: the map picture full width with Near You's list open beside a still copy of its button; on a phone the map picture too, the list under it; note 3's phone wording ("switch Near You to Map…") is gone.
  • Hide all routes moved left of the zoom buttons (a phone: top left); the route planner's note sits at the map's foot.
  • Fixed on the way: the "+N more systems below" count is worked out again when its line appears (the line takes room from the list); an opened Near You row keeps its reports in view, not only its first line; the names of a big gang are cut to 3 lines measured on the whole line (the narrow panel can start them on the line after the word), never while the panel is shut, and again when it opens.
  • Info text: intel's Near You line and [Map]; its cap 620 words (the owner: "raise it"). METHODOLOGY.md's Intel map paragraph.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #169: a list that is hidden by default must not be measured while hidden. The names cut (itNearCap) cached "it fits" for rows drawn while Near You's panel was shut (a hidden row is 0 px tall), so a long names list would have stayed uncut once the panel opened. Skip what can't be seen, key the cache on a width that is real (an inline element's clientWidth is always 0), and measure again when the panel opens.
