Why

  • Four backlog entries: a sovereignty filter (who holds each system), jumps in the past hour, kills in the past hour, and a region pick; and the 058 bug "the filter column on a phone is a jumble", which four more buttons would make worse.
  • The owner's answers (2026-11-20): Sov holder "Switch, default standing"; logos in the circles as in game, none over patches zoomed out ("No (Recommended)"); the hover "Show both"; Kills (past hour) "One filter, NPC as setting"; Region pick "Box per region + search", then a Select all box; the phone column "Fold the fix in"; the logo's ring "ring"; no distance fade for the three live colours ("ok, go with your choice for now."); soft colour "More". At the build review (2026-11-22): the sov field names "Fetch it once (Recommended)", one ESI kind "System activity", the phone column's wrap "Yes, build the wrap now".

What changed

  • The reader (L1): 057's itSovRead keeps every system's claim (itSov.info, itSovClaim, each field read defensively); itLvRead reads ESI's /universe/system_jumps/ and /universe/system_kills/ (IT_LV_URL, a new ESI kind activity, "System activity"). Each is read only while the live map shows its colour (or, for sov, 057's bridges need it), at most once per ESI's Expires, after a failure in 60 s, 120 s, then 5 minutes, in memory only, never on a timer of its own.
  • Three Colour filters: Sov holder (by your standings: yours, friendly, neutral, hostile, NPC faction space, unclaimed; or one colour per alliance in its settings; zoomed in, the holder's logo in each circle, ringed in its colour, a ★ on a capital, the ticker when the logo can't load; gates between two systems of one holder in its colour; the hover with the holding corporation, since when, the levels, ADM and the vulnerability window; an "In view" line under the legend). Jumps (past hour) 0 / 1–9 / 10–49 / 50–149 / 150–499 / 500+: grey for none, then a dim yellow rising to a vivid red (the owner at the hand test: "going from yellow to red. gray is none."). Kills (past hour) ships and pods 0 / 1 / 2–4 / 5–19 / 20+, or NPC kills 0 / 1–49 / 50–199 / 200–499 / 500+ in its settings. Before a read every system is grey and the legend says "no data yet", never 0; a system missing from an hour's list had none.
  • No distance fade for those three (Q10): only Only filters dim; the legend leaves out "faded" and "faint"; "Map: within"'s hover says so.
  • Soft colour 80% zoomed out and 62% in a zoomed-in disc (was 55% / 38%), for every colour.
  • Region pick, an Only filter: its list (a search ignoring dashes and spaces, Clear, Select all with a dash when some are picked, a box per region with its count, yours first); any pick counts; switched on with none picked, its list opens. The page's one checkbox rule gains the dash (:indeterminate).
  • The column by group: wrappers that are display:contents on a wide map (the column as before, with a 44 px gap when a short map wraps it, so a settings button doesn't cover the next column); on a phone Colour over View in one column and Only in the next, each wrapping on its own, 44 px rows, nothing moving when a settings button shows.
  • Stored: tvMapStack gains sovBy, killNpc and regions (names; one the table doesn't have is dropped). The older page reads the key as before (it ignores the new fields; a new colour or Region pick it doesn't know reads as Security and off); a save from the older page drops the new fields, so going back loses only those settings. Nothing from ESI is stored.
  • Info text: intel [Map] names the three ESI colours and Region pick (+41 words, cap 716).
  • Checks: new tools/page_checks/live_map_filters_check.py (1440 and 390 px; stand-ins for the three ESI lists with made-up holders and counts; 20 mutants). intel_check.py's ESI stand-in answers the hourly lists. check_request_gates.py lists itLvRead. intel_map_stack_check, intel_map_look_check and intel_map_layout_check follow the new column and soft colour.
