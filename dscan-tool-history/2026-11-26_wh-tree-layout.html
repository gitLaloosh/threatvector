Wormholes map: a tree that stays together

PageWormholes 2026-11-26 · v2026.11.26.2 · branch 073-wh-tree-layout · build queue 073 (plan roadmap/wh-tree-layout-plan.md, round 2; mockup Claude outputs/wh_tree_mockup.html)

Why

  • The owner (2026-11-25): "we need to fix this so the tree is drawn efficiently and easy to read". With Home locked off its tree place, only Home's neighbours followed it; every system two or more jumps from anything moved went back to its tree place at the map's origin, joined by long lines. New holes past a moved branch did the same.
  • At the build review the owner asked for the opposite of round 1's "a moved system brings its branch": "ideally, i'd like to be able to move a system w/out it automatically moving any others". He keeps landmarks (the k-space home, routed k-space systems, the J-space home, targets) locked and "outside of the big constantly changing tree of systems that are often deleted w/in 24hrs", and "most branches are going to start from the home system in j-space". This build session revised the plan and the mockup with his answers: "Auto-tidy, drag pins", "Small zoom, floor, pan", "Pick the direction", "This build session" (all "(Recommended)").

What changed

  • Two kinds of system: placed ones (moved, locked, or pinned by a drag) never move. Every other one grows from the nearest placed system above it in the tree (whPlaces), however many jumps away. A chain grows from Home (else your first k-space entry; whLayout). Nothing lands on another box: the next free row, found on a grid. The tree tidies itself as holes come and go.
  • A drag moves only what you drag: at the release, every other system the move itself would shift is pinned where it was (whTreePin: the map laid out with the dragged systems where they were, then where you dropped them). New holes grow from what you dragged.
  • The direction is left to right or top to bottom, whichever fits the free part of the map better (whTreeDir). The one in use stays until the other fits 1.25 times better; Rearrange picks afresh.
  • A new system you caused (a jump into it, + Add) whose place is out of view: a small zoom out to show it and the system it connects to, never under 0.7, else a pan (whShowNew, through whS.showNew in whPaint). It waits for the release of a drag or a pinch, as does the existing "you moved: brought into view".
  • Words: the whmap panel ("drag a system to move it alone", "Rearrange as a tree starts over from Home"; still 410 words), and the ⚙ panel's Rearrange note.
  • Stored data: none new. Pins go into tvWhChain.pos in its own form, so an older page reads them as moves.

Checks

  • New tools/page_checks/wormhole_tree_check.py (1440 and 390 px, real mouse drags), from the mockup's test_tree.py, with 8 mutants, all caught.
  • The mockup's own tests: 82 of 82; its 8 mutants caught. A ninth, a scan of every box instead of the grid, can't be told apart by time: 5.6 ms against 3.6 ms on 1,000 systems.

Lesson for future models

LESSON #185: a mockup that wraps a page function can hide what the page does around that call. The mockup's whSeen wrapper put the camera back after a jump, which hid whPaint's own re-centring in the middle of a drag. It was found while writing the page check; the page now holds that step while a gesture is under way.
