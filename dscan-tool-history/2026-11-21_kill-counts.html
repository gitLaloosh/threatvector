Why

  • The owner, 2026-11-20, after the first draft's kill rings: "maybe instead of changing rings, we just represent the death count by standing? amber X [N] [N] [N]", then "Yes, counts by who died (Recommended)", "Friendly, hostile, neutral (Recommended)" and, for the colours, "go with A".
  • Two items flagged for the delivery after 064: a hole removed from Intel's Wormholes list came back on the map because a Wormholes tab chain still named that system ("the wormhole tab chains should not force a hole to remain on the intel map. when it's gone, it's gone from the intel side."), and "all input fields that allow the entry of a system name should use the helper to identify real systems like the intel map does."

What changed

  • The map label's kill tag: the amber ✕, then one count per who died in its standing's colour, friendly, hostile, neutral (#4f8fe0 --side-friendly, #f08b7e, #b9c3ca), a standing with none left out ("✕ 3 5 2"). itMapReports counts kb: {f, h, n} from the kept kills' victim side (itKillBadges' rule: an unknown side is neutral), within "forget reports after"; the draw only writes the pieces (LESSON #120). The hover says it in words ("✕ 10 kills here: 3 friendlies lost, 5 hostiles killed, 2 neutrals killed", itMapKbTipHtml), the legend "✕ kills by who died: friendly hostile neutral". Rings, Near You's badges and Paths to you's tags unchanged.
  • The map's redraw key carries the three counts, not only the total (found at the build review: a re-work of sides can change who died without changing the total).
  • Your chain stays on the Wormholes tab (the owner: "B: chains off Intel's map (Recommended)"): 062's loop that marked your chain's k-space ends on Intel's map is gone; only Intel's own entries are drawn, so Remove in Intel's Wormholes list is final. Your own jumps between k-space and J-space still add an Intel entry (itWhJumpAdd), which the list removes.
  • Suggestions in five system boxes (Intel's Wormholes list → Add, Routes → Avoid; the Wormholes tab's + Add, "Add a system here" and Routes → Pin): each box is a .sys-cb with the map search's own list, laid over what's below it, bound by 064's sysComboBind the first time the box takes focus (sysCbBind, one focusin listener), so a redraw of their panels needs nothing more. J-space rows show the class. A pick fills the box; Add, Pin or Enter again acts as before (the owner: "Fill the box (Recommended)"). The "word = system" shorthand box, the paste boxes and the Feed search keep 064's reading (the owner: "Leave it out (Recommended)").
  • Info text: intel [Map]: "✕ counts kills by who died (friendly, hostile, neutral)", "(your chain's k-space ends too)" out, "are faded" to "fade": 675 of 675 words. METHODOLOGY.md's Intel map and chain paragraphs.
  • Checks: tools/page_checks/map_kill_counts_check.py (made-up kills through itNearAdd; every fillText on the map read back for the label's pieces and colours; the hover, the legend, Near You agreeing, the key; 9 mutants), tools/page_checks/system_boxes_check.py (each box: the list for "qxli", Enter fills and adds nothing, Enter again acts, an unknown name refused, the list on top and inside the window, 44 px rows at 390 px; 7 mutants), wormhole_filter_check.py's chain case rewritten (its mutant puts 062's loop back).

Lesson for future models

  • A redraw key has to carry what the drawing shows, not a summary of it: the map's key held each system's kill total, which stayed the same while the label's per-standing counts changed. LESSON #120's compare is only as good as its key.
  • A fix that keeps something "until it is seen again" needs a way to remove what has no row: the chain's marks had no line in Intel's list, so Remove could never reach them. Ask which side owns the data before patching the order of events.
