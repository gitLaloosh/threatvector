What changed

  • The filter: a new Only filter Sov hubs (IT_MAP_FILTERS hub, after Sov upgrades; IT_MAP_ONLY), its icon a hub of our own drawing (065's Sov holder has the flag the mockup used; LESSON #183) and its settings button "Sov hubs: which and when".
  • The Wormholes filter's icon is the game's wormhole shape (WH_SWIRL, as the Wormholes tab's signatures and end marks draw it; its definition moved before IT_MAP_FILTERS, which is built first).
  • The reader: itCamp / itCampNeed / itCampRead, ESI's /sovereignty/campaigns (IT_CAMP_URL, kind sov), read as 065's lists: only while Sov hubs is on and the live map shows (itMapLvWant('camp')), at most every 2 minutes (IT_CAMP_MS), memory only, 60 s / 120 s / 5 min after a failure, never on a timer of its own. itMapLvWant('sov') is true with Sov hubs on too, so the hubs' windows are read with any colour.
  • The match (itMapMatch, itHubIx, itHubAt): any ticked kind; a fight starting within its hours (0-48, 24 by default) or under way; a window open now or opening within its hours (0-24, now by default), never one that ended. Its key (itHubKey, also in itMapKey) holds the settings, whether each list is there, each read, and the minute (B2).
  • Its settings (058's panel itMp, IT_MP_KEYS): a tick and an RS stepper per kind (itMpHubRowHtml, RS.mapHubF, RS.mapHubV), a note, the count. Switched on the first time, they open (seen.hub).
  • The hover (itHubTipHtml, itHubFightHtml, itHubWhoHtml): each fight's kind, start or how long it has been under way, the defender and score (a freeport's alliances taking part), "nodes anywhere in this constellation"; the hub's window with how long is left, when it opens, or "ended"; "no vulnerability window (in a fight)". With Sov holder's colour on, its own window line gives way (B4).
  • The tags (itHubTags, in itMapLabels): "SOV 19:45" or "SOV now" beside fights in their hours that every Only filter picks out, with Fight coming up ticked; in a report's or a wormhole's label, or the name and tag where there's room.
  • The Only line (itHubOnlyText): "a sov fight within 24 h (N) or a hub vulnerable now (N) · ESI HH:MM", "under way" at 0 h, "no data yet", "no kind ticked".
  • Stored: tvMapStack gains only.hub, hub {fight, fightH, vuln, vulnH} and seen.hub. A stack from before reads with Sov hubs off and the defaults; bad hours fall back; the page from main drops the new fields and keeps the rest.
  • CARD_INFO.intel [Map]: "Sov hubs: sov fights coming up and vulnerable hubs, from ESI"; the Intel cap 765, from the final count. METHODOLOGY.md: Sov hubs.
  • Checks: tools/page_checks/sov_hubs_check.py (new; 12 mutants), a campaigns stand-in in intel_check.py; the filter lists in intel_map_stack_check, live_map_filters_check, sov_upgrades_check, intel_map_look_check and paths_to_you_check; check_request_gates.py lists itCampRead and IT_CAMP_URL.
