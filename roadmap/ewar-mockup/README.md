# Mockup: EWAR in the fight (all three deliveries)

For the Project doc `claude/roadmap/ewar-plan.md` ([stated] Q6 "ok" to "one mockup covering all three deliveries"). Made 2026-11-13 by the research session from `main` bd04bb5 (v2026.11.13.2); the icon badges (plan section 11) added 2026-11-14. Sent to the owner as `ewar_mockup.html`; in the repo's `Claude outputs/` since phase A's delivery (build queue 050, v2026.11.18.1): screenshots of the real page in four states (Today, A, A + B, A + B + C) with buttons to switch, the badge styles, and a phone section. Phase A built TD / GD and the badges (faint frame); B (neuts and nos) and C (damps, lock range, lock time, ECM) are not built yet.

- `fits.py`: the two made-up fits. Threat: Arbitrator "Mockup EWAR" (2 Tracking Disruptor II with the optimal and tracking scripts, Remote Sensor Dampener II with the range script, Multispectral ECM II, 2 Medium Energy Neutralizer II, Small Energy Nosferatu II, Hobgoblin II ×5). Yours: Thorax "Mockup blaster" (5 Heavy Neutron Blaster II with Void M, Medium Armor Repairer II, AB, scram, web, Cap Recharger II, Hammerhead II ×2).
- `proto_ewar.js`: the feature as prototyped, injected after the page shows both fits. It wraps page functions; the page's own code draws everything.
  - A: `pyfaApplicationMult` gets your turrets' optimal / falloff / tracking times the TDs' stacking-penalised multipliers at each distance (pyfa Effect6424 maths, range factor from `pyfaCalculateRangeFactor`). `dmgRangeMarks` adds D zones for their TD reach.
  - B: `ttkDefense` gets your capacitor's empty time from the page's `capSimPyfa` with your modules' drains (`estimate.capacitor.simInputs.withoutBoosts`) plus each neut and nos cycle at the fight range (pyfa `addDrain`).
  - C: lock range counts for both ships (`ignoreLockRange` off in `pyfaApplicationMult`, `pyfaGetDroneMult`, `pyfaGetFighterAbilityMult`, `pyfaProjectedPoint`), yours damped by their dampener; the lock pin says "damped"; D zones for the damp and ECM reach; lock times (pyfa `calculateLockTime`) and jam chance (pyfa `jamChance`) in the Matchup's "why" panels.
  - A line under each chart (`.proto-ewar-line`, after the chart key) and extra sentences in `mysMatchup`'s `why` texts.
  - Not in the mockup: resistances (neither hull has one), guidance disruptors (no missiles on either fit). Phase A's build does both (`tools/page_checks/ewar_disrupt_check.py`).
- Icon badges (plan section 11): `P.iconize` puts the module's icon (`estimate.moduleStates.items` typeId, farthest-reaching running module of the zone's kind) on each chart badge and key row; `P.badgeStyle` 'thin' (faint frame, the owner's pick), 'bar' (bar under the icon) or 'off' (today's letters); the letter stays underneath in the category colour; `P.highlight` links a badge and its key row (mouseover). Icons come from pyfa's copy through esi_from_sde's `icon_route`.
- `build_ewar.py <repo>/index.html ewar_mockup.html [shots dir]`: needs `PYFA_SRC` (pyfa v2.70.0 checkout) for the repo's `tools/page_checks/esi_from_sde.py` (ESI answered from pyfa's copy of CCP's data, no network). Fight range 4 km. Prints 8 checks (no page errors; no sideways scroll at 1440 / 390 px; A lowers your DPS; B shortens "you last", A doesn't; their DPS unchanged; the line under their chart from B on; C's lock and jam sentences; every key-row icon loaded). The copy in `Claude outputs/` was built from main c35a671, before phase A; on a later page the prototype's wrappers would draw on top of what phase A draws itself.
- The 22 screenshots in the HTML were each looked at (made-up fits, no names or paths) and are listed in `tools/pii_reviewed_images.json`.

Numbers at 4 km (2026-11-13, the prototype): your DPS on them 240 → 65 (A); they last 1 min 17 s → 4 min 52 s (A); you last 1 min 12 s → 58 s (B: capacitor empties at 0:30 instead of 1:32); your lock range 65.6 → 40.5 km; their ECM 18% per cycle; lock times 3.7 s both ways (C). The built phase A gives the same 65 and 4 min 52 s.

Run: `PYFA_SRC=/path/to/pyfa python3 -I build_ewar.py <repo>/index.html ewar_mockup.html shots`
