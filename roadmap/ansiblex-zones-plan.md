## How the game works (CCP; see Sources)
- A jump's capacitor cost = ship class × the zone multiplier. The zone is set by the light-years from the jump's **endpoint** (where you arrive) to the **owning alliance's capital**: zone 1 0–5 LY ×0 (free), 2 5.1–10 ×2, 3 10.1–15 ×6, 4 15.1–20 ×9, 5 20.1+ ×15. How far apart the two bridges are doesn't matter.
- Only pilots of the alliance that holds sov over the jump's **starting** system can use it; capitals can't.
- An alliance with no capital (lost; 30 days to set a new one) pays zone 5 on every jump.
- So one bridge can be free one way and cost the other: the zone of each direction is the zone of the system it arrives in. The owner's rule ("not outside zone 1 unless jumping back into zone 1") is "use a direction only when its arrival is in a ticked zone", with zone 1 ticked.

## Changes
1. **Read EVE's sovereignty** (`itSovRead()`, new): `GET https://esi.evetech.net/sovereignty/systems?compatibility_date=2026-05-19` through `esiFetch` (new kind `sov`, so the header's budget gauge counts it), a new constant `IT_SOV_URL`.
   - Why not `${ESI}`: the route isn't under `/latest` (404 there), and it needs a compatibility date of 2026-05-19 or later (404 with 2025-08-26). The date as a **query parameter** keeps it a simple GET: no extra header, so no CORS preflight (the answer carries `access-control-allow-origin: *`). **D1:** the query form, not `X-Compatibility-Date` (both answered 200 in the owner's browser; the header would make every call two).
   - Kept **in memory only** (`itSov = {sys: Map<system id, alliance id>, caps: Map<alliance id, capital system id>, at, exp}`): about 1.27 MB of JSON, about 76 KB on the wire, public CCP data, never stored. **D2:** no storage: capitals change at most every 30 days, but who holds a system can change any time.
   - **When:** only while Intel runs **and** 041's "Use jump bridges on the map" is on **and** 041's list has a bridge (a ticked zone isn't needed: change 11 uses it for the map's counts too); on the first route search that needs it, then again at most once per ESI's `Expires` (5 minutes), and only when a route is worked out (no timer of its own: LESSON #120).
